🦈 Vista Del Mar Community Hub & Parents AI Toolkit
An unofficial, community-managed hub and AI resource center for Vista Del Mar (VdM) families—combining whole-school master calendar automation, custom multi-child bell schedule generators, and a curated AI toolkit for parents and caregivers.

🌟 Key Features
📅 1. 2-Step Calendar Sync System
Step 1: Live Master Mako Calendar (Auto-Sync): Continuous real-time sync with all scraped Mako Messenger newsletters (Smore), principal updates, PTA events, minimum days, and official Capo USD school holidays.
Step 2: Custom Family Bell Schedule Builder:
Supports multi-child setups across TK (Early & Late Birds), Kindergarten, 1st–3rd, 4th–5th, and Middle School.
Choose between Combined Drop-off/Dismissal Invites or Separate Invites Per Child.
Automatic Break Exclusions: Automatically skips Thanksgiving, Winter Recess, Spring Break, and all legal holidays (EXDATE).
100% Private: Built locally inside each family's browser—no child names or schedules are ever shared with other users.
💡 2. Parents & Caregivers AI Hub
Life-Changing Parent Prompts: Tested, copy-paste ready AI prompts for Mental Load Audits, Newsletter Sifting, Morning Routines, 30-Min Dinners, Homework Analogies, Meltdown-Free Transitions, Screen Time Agreements, and Memory Journaling.
Curated Family AI Tools: Direct access to Google Gemini, Google NotebookLM, Gemini for Kids, BedtimeStory.ai, PinwheelGPT, and LittleLit AI.
Chrome AI Skills Guide: Step-by-step guide to saving parent prompts directly into Chrome's Ask Gemini side panel.
📬 3. Community Connect & Creator Support
Unified Submission Form: Submit missing school events, inquire about sponsorships & ads, or consult on custom AI workflows.
Venmo Tip Link: Direct support link for the community project creator.
