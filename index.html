Invisible Mental Load Dump

Organization

Audit mental clutter into automated tasks, delegation scripts, and a low-stress schedule.

"I am carrying a heavy mental load. Here is a brain dump of what is in my head: [insert bullet points]. Please audit this list and deliver: (1) what I can systemize or automate, (2) what I can delegate, (3) what can wait for next week, and (4) a realistic, low-stress schedule for the rest."

School Email & Newsletter Sifter

School Comms

Instantly extracts dates, backpack checklists, and permission slip deadlines from long newsletters.

"I am going to paste a long school newsletter/email chain. Extract all key actions and organize them into a clear table with columns: Date, Event, Affected Child, Action Required, and Deadline. Also provide a 'Backpack Checklist' of physical items needed."

Dinner Decision-Maker (Zero-Waste)

Meal Prep

Creates a 5-day weeknight dinner plan under 30 minutes active prep time with deconstructed options for picky eaters.

"Act as a zero-waste family chef. Create a 5-day dinner plan for [2 adults and 2 kids]. Keep prep under 30 minutes, use overlapping ingredients to eliminate food waste, provide 'deconstructed' options for picky eaters, and generate a categorized grocery list."
